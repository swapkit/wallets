Bitget Wallet now connects NEAR, Starknet, Sui, TON and XRP, and every EVM chain it lists. Chains are connected one at a time, so a chain missing in the wallet is skipped instead of failing the whole connection. Cosmos is no longer offered with Bitget for now: the wallet refuses the Cosmos Hub connection request.
